Interpretation:
- The approach is solved: never_reached is 0 at 20k on nominal (run 7 at 6k: 3). The
  remaining nominal failures are grasping (grasp_missed 10, dropped 4), i.e.
  fine alignment/closing, not localisation.
- Shifted 20 %: 31/50 never_reached. The policy still does not extrapolate to cube poses
  outside the training box. DART noise (std 0.3, about 6 mm per step) widens the state
  distribution around the demo trajectories but does not add new cube positions. To
  move this number, the training pose box would have to grow.
- The loss is still falling (0.084 at 12k, 0.07 at 20k), and the dev rate is noisy
  across checkpoints, so "best checkpoint" selection on 50 dev episodes is itself
  noisy. A larger dev set, or averaging over checkpoints/seeds, would be needed to tell
  run 7 and run 8 apart.
- Demo seeds 0-9: 5/10, lower than dev but n=10 (23.7-76.3 %), so not meaningful.

Caveats: one training seed. The 20k checkpoint was chosen on the same dev seeds (so 72 %
is slightly optimistic). Dev seeds 10500-10549 have now been evaluated on many policies
and should not serve as a final test (seeds 11000+ are untouched).

DATA (record_run8.py)
---------------------
- 200 scripted-expert episodes on demo seeds 0-199, nominal pose box. 96 px front + wrist
  as LeRobot video (repo LeRobotWriter, use_videos=True), 15-D observation.state, and the
  5-D absolute-target action [target_x, target_y, target_z, target_yaw, gripper]. Same
  features as run 7.
- DART on every odd seed (100 episodes): Gaussian noise with std 0.3 (normalised action
  units) added to the EXECUTED delta on dims x, y, z, yaw only (indices 0, 1, 2, 5),
  then clipped to [-1, 1]. Roll, pitch and the gripper are untouched. Noise RNG is
  np.random.default_rng(1_000_000 + seed). The expert keeps reading the env and corrects.
- Label = the CLEAN expert intent at every step: the target the expert's own action
  would produce from the current integrated target, mirroring env._integrate_target:
  pos = clip(cur + a[:3]*0.02, workspace), yaw = clip(wrap(cur_yaw + a[5]*0.1), +-pi/2),
  plus the expert's gripper command. On clean episodes this label equals
  env.target_pose() after the step to 3.0e-8 (checked on every frame).
- Only successful episodes are written. Per group:
    clean (even seeds): 100/100 success, 11020 frames kept
    DART  (odd seeds):   98/100 success (1 dropped, seed 143; 1 collision, seed 175),
                         11157 frames kept
  Total 198 episodes, 22177 frames (4.0x run 7's 5513). Recording wall 205.5 s.
  Per-attempt log: results/attempts.jsonl; summary: results/recording_summary.json.
- Clean-label replay through AbsoluteTargetWrapper on the 100 un-noised seeds
  (verify_replay8.py): 100/100 success, every episode the recorded length
  (results/replay_clean_labels.json).
